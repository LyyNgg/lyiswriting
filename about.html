About

Hi, I'm ly. I think about products for a living and can't stop doing it after hours.

This is where I write down what I notice while using apps: the onboarding flows, the pricing pages, the tiny details that make something feel effortless, and the moments where it falls apart.

Every post looks at an app through the same questions: who it's for, what job it does, how it gets people to the "aha" moment, how it makes money, and what I'd change.
