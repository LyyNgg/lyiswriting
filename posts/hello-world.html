Why I'm writing about apps

What this blog is, and the framework I'll use to look at every product.

Every app I open is a stack of decisions someone made: what to show first, what to ask for, what to charge, what to leave out. Most of the time we only feel those decisions. This blog is my attempt to name them.

The lens

Each post walks through the same set of questions, so the posts are easy to compare:

  1. The job. What is someone actually hiring this app to do?
  2. First impression. How fast does it get me to value? Where is the "aha" moment?
  3. Core loop. What brings me back, and is it earned or engineered?
  4. Business model. How does it make money, and does that align with what's good for me?
  5. Friction. Where did I get stuck, confused, or annoyed?
  6. What I'd build next. If I were the PM, what would I prioritize and how would I measure it?
Ground rule: I only write about apps I've actually used for real, not just downloaded for a screenshot.
