The noodle bar that only opens at 2 a.m.

We spent a week with the cooks, cab drivers and night-shift nurses who keep it full.

Mira Okafor
October 3, 2026
hero photo — steam over the counter
SHARE

The sign goes on at 1:58. By 2:05 there's a line of twelve people, half of them still in scrubs, and the first pot of broth has been simmering since before dinner service ended across the street.

Nobody remembers who started it. The owner, a former line cook named Daniel, says the hours chose him: "Everyone I cooked with was hungry after midnight, and there was nowhere to go."

"Everyone I cooked with was hungry after midnight."

On a Tuesday the room fits about twenty. On Friday it fits whoever's willing to stand.
